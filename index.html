About Me

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at Chungbuk National University (CBNU) , where I am a member of the Information System Lab (ISL) under the supervision of Prof. Ohyun Jo .

My primary research interest is Time Series Forecasting, with a particular focus on multivariate time series modeling, channel dependency, correlation, and aperiodic temporal components.
